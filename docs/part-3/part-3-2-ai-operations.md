## 1. Operator AI Copilot & Cross-Module Reasoning (Section 30 & 31)

The AI Copilot synthesizes real-time signals across CRM, Telemetry, GIS, and Incidents to recommend concrete operator actions:
1. **Root-Cause Hypothesis Generation:** Correlates multiple customer complaints to an upstream fiber cut.
2. **Safe Action Recommendations:** Proposes Ping diagnostics, port toggles, or soft reboots with risk-tier badges.
3. **Approval Gating:** Prompts operators with step-up verification dialogs before triggering high-risk mutations.
