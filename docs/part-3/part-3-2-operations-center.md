## 1. Unified Operations Command Center (Section 4 & 6)

The Operations Command Center aggregates real-time data across all operational domains:
- **Broadband Fleet Health:** Active subscribers, online/offline ONT devices, degraded optical power percentage.
- **Incident & Alarm Radar:** Open fiber cuts, correlated PON alarms, estimated affected customer count.
- **Support & SLA Queue:** Open tickets, impending SLA breach count, unassigned jobs.
- **Field Technician Status:** Active dispatches, jobs in-progress, completed repairs pending verification.
- **AI Copilot Insights:** Pending high-risk action approvals (reboots, WAN modifications), automated diagnostic recommendations.
