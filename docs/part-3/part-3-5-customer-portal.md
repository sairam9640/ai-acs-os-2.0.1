## 1. Customer Self-Service Portal (Section 4 & 7)

The customer portal provides a clean, subscriber-facing view:
- **Broadband Health Gauge:** Real-time connection status (`ONLINE`, `DEGRADED`, `OFFLINE`, `MAINTENANCE`).
- **Wi-Fi Manager:** Displays primary SSID, connected wireless client count, and allows self-service password updates.
- **Invoices & Billing:** Displays current account balance, downloadable PDF invoices, and one-click payment settlement.
- **Support & Tickets:** Submits support issues, tracks field technician dispatch ETA, and interacts with the AI support copilot.
