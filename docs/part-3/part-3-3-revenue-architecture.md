## 1. Revenue Accounting Principles (Section 28 & 29)

Revenue accounting strictly separates billed, collected, and outstanding amounts:
- **Total Billed:** Sum of all issued invoices within period.
- **Total Collected:** Actual received funds verified by payment gateway settlements.
- **Outstanding / Overdue:** Invoices past due date without payment.
- **Net Revenue Formula:**
  $$\text{Net Collected Revenue} = \text{Collected Payments} - \text{Refunds} - \text{Applied Credits}$$
