## 1. Skill Matrix & Dispatch Qualification (Section 16 & 17)

Technicians are assigned based on verified certifications:
- `FIBER_SPLICING`: Fusion splicer certified (required for FAT/ODF repairs).
- `ONT_PROVISIONING`: TR-069 / USP provisioning certified.
- `WIFI_SURVEY`: RF heatmapping & multi-AP mesh installation.
- `OLT_MAINTENANCE`: High-risk central office chassis operations.
