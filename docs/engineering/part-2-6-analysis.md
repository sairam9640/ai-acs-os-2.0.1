## 1. Executive Summary & Full System Synthesis

Part 2.6 synthesizes the complete **AI ISP OS platform**, uniting the 33 screens from Part 1 with the hardened backend, network, fiber, and AI engines from Part 2.1–2.5 into an integrated production-ready frontend experience across Super Admin, Operator, Technician, and Customer portals.

### Application Suite:
1. **Super Admin Portal:** Multi-tenant platform orchestration, global revenue aggregates, tenant lifecycle provisioning, and platform health metrics.
2. **Operator Portal & Customer 360:** Subscriber CRM, ONT hardware fleet management, interactive Fiber GIS route visualizer, incident management, and AI Copilot.
3. **Technician Mobile App:** Priority job queue with SLA tracking, turn-by-turn GIS navigation to pole FAT boxes, optical power measurement entry, and offline evidence capture.
4. **Customer Self-Service App:** Live broadband service status, Wi-Fi configuration management, ticket submission, and customer AI troubleshooting assistant.
5. **Universal 6 UI States:** Loading skeleton, empty state, permission denied, capability unavailable, async command in-flight, and verified success state.
